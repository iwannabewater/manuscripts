## Notes On Use

- RMB figures are reported as disclosed by the source. USD conversions in source releases are not used for cross-company comparison.
- Q1 2026 comparisons use each company's own delivery, revenue, margin, cash and guidance definitions.
- Delivery data are company-reported and not adjusted for registration or insurance data.
- This is a strategy and industry analysis, not investment advice.
