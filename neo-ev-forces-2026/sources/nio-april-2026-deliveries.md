## NIO Inc. Achieves 22.8% YoY Growth with 29,356 Deliveries in April

In April 2026, NIO Inc. delivered 29,356 vehicles, representing an increase of 22.8% year-over-year. The deliveries consisted of 19,024 vehicles from NIO's premium smart electric vehicle brand NIO, 5,352 vehicles from the family-oriented smart electric vehicle brand ONVO, up 21.6% year-over-year; and 4,980 vehicles from the small smart high-end electric car brand firefly. In the first four months of 2026, NIO Inc. has delivered a cumulative total of 112,821 vehicles, marking a 71.0% increase year-over-year. The ONVO L80, a large five-seat flagship SUV, launches on May 15. The NIO ES9, a flagship executive SUV, follows in late May, with deliveries commencing then.
