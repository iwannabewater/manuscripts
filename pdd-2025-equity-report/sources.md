## Method Boundaries

- This report uses PDD Holdings Inc. as the reporting entity. It does not treat the Pinduoduo app and Temu as separately reported profit centers because PDD reports one operating segment.
- RMB amounts in the report are generally presented in 亿元人民币. Minor rounding differences may appear because source statements are in RMB thousands or millions.
- The DCF and P/E target price are author estimates. They are not sourced facts and should not be read as investment advice.
- The report does not infer GMV, buyer count, Temu revenue, or regional profitability because PDD does not disclose those items in the 2025 Form 20-F.
