## Method Boundaries

- PDD reports one consolidated commerce group and does not publish Temu revenue, GMV, marketing spend, logistics cost, or profitability. Any Temu or domestic-main-app interpretation in this report is an inference from revenue mix, management commentary, policy events, and external analyst summaries.
- Public analyst-reaction sources disagree slightly on consensus estimates: Reuters/LSEG cites RMB109.33 billion revenue consensus; Investing.com cites RMB109.82 billion and RMB16.77 non-GAAP EPS consensus. The report uses both with source attribution and focuses on direction: revenue miss was modest, EPS miss was large.
- Stock-price data are from Investing.com historical prices at the time of source check. Intraday/current prices may change after 2026-05-29.
- This report is not investment advice and does not provide a formal buy/sell recommendation.
