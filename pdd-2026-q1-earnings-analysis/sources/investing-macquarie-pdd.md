Investing.com - Macquarie downgraded Pinduoduo Inc. (NASDAQ:PDD) to Neutral from Outperform on Thursday and slashed its price target to $87 from $151, citing challenges in achieving a near-term earnings recovery. The stock currently trades at $86.61, hovering near its 52-week low of $83.61, with shares down 24% year-to-date.

The company reported first-quarter fiscal 2026 revenue of Rmb106 billion, an 11% increase, but adjusted net profit fell 17% year-over-year. The quarter included a Rmb2 billion investment loss.

Online marketing services revenue growth slowed to 2% year-over-year in the first quarter from 5% in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, indicating continued take rate contraction and less favorable market share dynamics. Sales and marketing spending rose to 32% of total revenue from 29% in fiscal 2025.

Pinduoduo officially launched its new first-party product brand "New Pinmu" during the quarter. Management expects to spend more than Rmb100 billion over the next three years to build another platform comparable to its existing business.

The stock trades at 8.8 times fiscal 2026 estimated price-to-earnings, compared with 10 to 12 times for industry peers. Macquarie cited lackluster consumption, aggressive e-commerce competition, and continued investment needs for supply chains and overseas expansion as headwinds. Despite near-term pressures, InvestingPro analysis suggests the stock is undervalued at current levels, with the company maintaining a "GREAT" financial health score of 3.49 out of 5.

In other recent news, Pinduoduo Inc. reported its first-quarter 2026 earnings, revealing a significant shortfall in both earnings per share (EPS) and revenue compared to analyst forecasts. The company posted an EPS of 9.51 RMB, which was below the expected 16.77 RMB, and reported revenue of 106.2 billion RMB, falling short of the anticipated 109.82 billion RMB. Following these results, several financial firms adjusted their price targets for Pinduoduo. Morgan Stanley lowered its price target to $129 from $148, citing a reduction in earnings per share estimates by up to 14% for the coming years. US Tiger Securities also adjusted its target to $100 from $110, attributing the change to mixed first-quarter results. Jefferies followed suit by reducing its price target to $121 from $146 while maintaining a Buy rating, noting the softer-than-expected earnings. These developments reflect analysts’ responses to Pinduoduo’s recent financial performance.
