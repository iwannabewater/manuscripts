Investing.com - Barclays downgraded Pinduoduo Inc. (NASDAQ:PDD) to Equalweight from Overweight and lowered its price target to $89 from $165. The stock currently trades at $86.61, near its 52-week low of $83.61, though InvestingPro data suggests the company remains undervalued with a Fair Value of $109.76.

Analyst Jiong Shao said first-quarter results showed the core China business was weaker than expected while Temu growth accelerates. The firm noted that Pinduoduo historically had an industry-high monetization rate in China and those take rates are now under pressure as Chinese regulators have become increasingly protective of small merchants. According to InvestingPro Tips, three analysts have revised their earnings downwards for the upcoming period, though the company still posted revenue growth of nearly 10% over the last twelve months.

Barclays highlighted that Pinduoduo launched a 100 billion yuan merchant support fund in April last year. With lower take rates, China revenue growth rate is likely to be below its GMV growth rate, and limited China revenue growth may remain quite low in the coming quarters.

The company has also entered the private label market, which may compete with its merchant sellers in categories where Pinduoduo’s private label products sit. Internationally, after some hiccups due to tariffs, Temu GMV growth has recovered and is now re-accelerating, but the business is likely to be loss-making in the near to medium term.

Barclays said it is moving to the sideline with slowing China business and uncertainty around private label entry.

In other recent news, Pinduoduo Inc. has reported mixed first-quarter results for fiscal 2026, with revenue reaching RMB106.2 billion, marking an 11% increase year-over-year. However, the company’s adjusted net profit fell by 17% year-over-year, partly due to a Rmb2 billion investment loss. Goldman Sachs responded by lowering its price target for Pinduoduo to $145, citing an earnings miss and maintaining a Buy rating. Similarly, Morgan Stanley reduced its price target to $129 while keeping an Overweight rating, also due to missed earnings expectations. Macquarie downgraded Pinduoduo to Neutral from Outperform, slashing its price target to $87, pointing to challenges in achieving a near-term earnings recovery. US Tiger Securities adjusted its price target to $100, maintaining a Hold rating, based on the mixed quarterly results. Additionally, Jefferies cut its price target to $121, reflecting softer-than-expected quarterly earnings while maintaining a Buy rating. These developments highlight the varied analyst responses to Pinduoduo’s recent financial performance.
